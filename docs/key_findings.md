# Key Findings: LexEuropa Legal Group (2024–2026)

**Dashboard:** [`LexEuropa_Legal_Dashboard.pbix`](../LexEuropa_Legal_Dashboard.pbix)
**Data:** 15,000 legal cases, 200 lawyers, 800 clients and 15 offices across Europe, opened January 2024 – December 2026. All money is in **euros (€)**.

| Firm at a glance (2024–2026) | |
|---|---|
| Cases | 15,000 |
| Revenue | €2.80B |
| Profit | €1.59B |
| Average profit margin | 49.6% |
| Win rate (won ÷ decided cases) | 38.5% |
| Outstanding balance | €773M (27.6% of invoiced) |

> **How win rate is measured:** won cases ÷ decided cases (won + lost + settled). Ongoing and withdrawn cases are excluded.

---

## 1. Growth: how is the firm trending?

| Year | Cases | Revenue | Profit | Avg margin | Win rate | Avg risk score |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | 4,407 | €620M | €352M | 49.5% | 39.9% | 40.7 |
| 2025 | 5,375 | €1,109M | €629M | 49.7% | 39.7% | 42.1 |
| 2026 | 5,218 | €1,074M | €611M | 49.7% | **34.6%** | 42.3 |

- **2025 was the growth year:** cases +22% and revenue +79% compared with 2024.
- **2026 levelled off:** cases -2.9%, revenue -3.1% and profit -2.9% compared with 2025.
- **Profit margin is extremely stable** at 49.5–49.7% every year.
- **Win rate fell sharply in 2026**, from 39.7% to 34.6%. Treat this with care: 45% of 2026 cases are still ongoing (vs 11–13% in earlier years), so the cases decided so far may not be typical. The drop appears at every seniority level.

| Mergers & Acquisitions | 1,874 | €423.4M | €226K | 38.1% |

| Corporate Law | 2,141 | €347.1M | €162K | 39.7% |

| … | | | | |

- **Regulatory Compliance and M&A lead on revenue**, and they also earn the most per case.
- **Family Law brings in the least revenue only because it has the fewest cases.** Its revenue per case (€171K) is mid-table, and it has the best win rate.
- **Profit margin is almost identical in every practice area** (49.3%–49.9%). The differences between areas come from volume and case size, not efficiency.
- **Top offices:** Frankfurt (€365.6M) and London (€358.6M), then Paris (€292.0M).
- **Top client region: Western Europe (€1,020.8M)**, then Northern Europe (€834.3M). Western Europe leads in every year.
- **Seasonality:** cases opened in September–November bring in the most revenue. October is the strongest month overall (€369.2M across the three years) and the single best month was October 2025 (€172.9M).

## 3. Risk, complexity and duration: what drives profitability?

| Complexity | Cases | Total profit | Profit per case | Margin | Avg risk | Avg days open |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | 802 | €904.4M | €1.13M | **59.9%** | 92.7 | 440 |
| High | 2,996 | €480.2M | €160K | 55.1% | 73.0 | 356 |
| Medium | 5,309 | €174.5M | €33K | 50.1% | 45.5 | 263 |
| Low | 5,893 | €33.3M | €6K | **45.0%** | 15.6 | 180 |

- **Complex, high-risk, long-running cases are the most profitable.** Critical cases are just 5% of volume but produce **57% of total profit**, at a 60% margin.
- **Higher risk means a higher margin, not a lower one.** Margin rises steadily from 45.0% (risk score 0–20) to 57.8% (risk score 80–100).
- **Priority follows the same pattern:** Urgent cases average €901K revenue, against €322K for High, €69K for Medium and €24K for Low.

## 4. Lawyers: outcomes, workload and seniority

| Seniority | Lawyers | Cases | Revenue | Revenue per case | Win rate | Satisfaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Partner | 12 | 516 | €667.3M | €1.29M | 44.1% | 6.7 |
| Counsel | 35 | 2,690 | €1,394.2M | €518K | 43.1% | 6.8 |
| Senior Associate | 64 | 5,163 | €559.7M | €108K | 39.9% | 6.7 |
| Associate | 89 | 6,631 | €182.0M | €27K | 35.0% | 6.5 |

- **Counsel generate the most revenue overall (€1.39B).** Partners earn by far the most per case.
- **Associates' low revenue per case reflects the cases they're given, not their efficiency.** 71% of Associate cases are Low complexity, and they handle no High or Critical cases. Partners handle only High and Critical cases.
- **Win rate rises with seniority and age:** from 35.3% (age 25–34) to 43.7% (65+).
- **Satisfaction barely differs by seniority** (6.5–6.8). Partners don't have a satisfaction problem.
- **Workload sits with Partners.** They average 43.8% utilisation and a workload index of 2.64, against 15.3% and 0.97 for Associates. All 12 lawyers above 40% utilisation are Partners. Average utilisation across the firm almost doubled in 2026 (15.9% → 29.4%).
- **By age group:** 45–54 (59 lawyers, 4,397 cases) produces the most profit (€780M), followed by 55–64 (18 lawyers, 1,291 cases, €444M). The largest group is 35–44 (71 lawyers, 5,572 cases).
- **Top performer:** Mathieu Lindqvist (LWY-1026, Partner, Corporate Law), with a performance score of 0.98: 50 cases, €65.4M revenue, 52.8% win rate and 7.3 satisfaction. The score is 40% normalised revenue + 30% normalised win rate + 30% normalised satisfaction.

### The gender profit gap is a seniority gap

Male lawyers (112) generated €1,026.6M of profit and female lawyers (85) €539.8M; 3 non-binary lawyers generated €25.9M. **Within each seniority level, profit per case is almost the same for every gender.** For example, female Partners average €777K per case and male Partners €764K. The gap comes from who holds senior roles: **men hold 9 of the 12 Partner roles and 22 of the 35 Counsel roles.**

## 5. Clients: who contributes the most value?

- **Revenue is spread widely across clients.** The top 10 clients contribute just 4.1% of revenue, and the top 10% of clients contribute 23.7%. There's no dangerous dependence on a few clients.
- **Large (€451.0M) and Mid-Market (€420.8M) clients produce the most profit, simply because there are more of them.** Profit per case is similar for every company size (€101K–€116K). Multinationals produce the least profit (€156.3M) because they're the smallest group (81 clients), not because they're less profitable.
- **Strategic clients** (132 clients, 16.5% of the base) bring in €560M (20% of revenue).
- **€773M (27.6% of invoiced fees) is still unpaid.**

## 6. Client satisfaction: what drives it?

| Case outcome | Avg satisfaction (0–10) |
|---|---|
| Won | **8.51** |
| Ongoing | 7.00 |
| Settled | 6.49 |
| Withdrawn | 4.98 |
| Lost | **3.52** |

**Satisfaction depends almost entirely on the outcome.** It has virtually no link with profit margin, risk score, case duration or revenue (correlation 0.02–0.05 for each), and it barely changes with complexity (6.55–6.73). Clients judge the firm on results, not on cost or speed.

---

## Recommended actions

1. **Grow Critical and High complexity work.** It earns 55–60% margins and most of the profit. This means more Partner and Counsel capacity.
2. **Relieve Partner workload.** All of the most-utilised lawyers are Partners, and firm-wide utilisation nearly doubled in 2026. Delegate Medium-complexity work to Senior Associates.
3. **Develop Associates into higher-value work.** Pair them with Partners and Counsel on High-complexity cases, since their low revenue comes from case mix, not performance.
4. **Address the gender gap through promotion, not case allocation.** Profit per case is equal within each level, so review progression into Counsel and Partner roles.
5. **Investigate the 2026 win-rate drop** once more 2026 cases close. Start with Intellectual Property, the practice area with the lowest win rate.
6. **Collect outstanding fees.** €773M, 27.6% of invoiced fees, is unpaid.
7. **Plan capacity for the September–November peak.**
8. **Protect win rate to protect satisfaction.** Lost cases average 3.5 out of 10, so managing client expectations on weak cases (for example, settling early) is the main lever for satisfaction.
